# Dee Chess

Play chess against bots while a coach pauses on your mistakes and gives less help as your rank rises.

**Play:** https://patulokod.github.io/dee-chess/ — open it once with internet, then use *Add to Home screen*. After that it works offline.

## What it does

- **Ranks:** Initiate → Squire → Tactician → Strategist → Sovereign → Dee One. At the first two ranks the coach tells you what was wrong, why, and what to play. From Tactician up it only tells you what and why. At Dee One it only says "Wrong."
- **9 bots** from 400 to 2200, matched to your rating, plus 3 placement games.
- **Your mistakes become puzzles:** every position where the coach stopped you comes back after 1, 3 and 7 days.
- **"What's the threat?":** after some opponent moves, you tap the piece in danger. It builds the habit that stops blunders.
- Everything runs on the phone. Progress is saved in the browser.
